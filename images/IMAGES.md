# NexaTech: images to find

Put every file in the `images/` folder next to `index.html`, using the exact filename. Missing images show a grey placeholder, so you can add them gradually.

**Sizes:** JPG, about 1600px on the long side for large images and 800px for products. Aim for under 300KB each. Images are cropped to fill their frame, so keep the subject centred.

**Where to look:** Unsplash and Pexels (free licences, check each image's terms), or brand press and media pages for official product photos. Only use images you own or are allowed to use.

**Style guide:** Site and hero images look best dark and moody with cool blue light, matching the charcoal and electric blue palette. Product photos should be clean, on a plain white or light grey background, with no watermark or text.

### Extra gallery images (optional)

The product details page has four gallery slots. For any product you can add `p<id>-2.jpg`, `p<id>-3.jpg` and `p<id>-4.jpg` (for example `p1-2.jpg`). Good ideas: the back, the side, the screen on, and the product in use. The main `p<id>.jpg` is the first slot.
